I graduated from a top 10 CS program. But fast forward today, I'm helping construction firms close $20,000,000+ in bids. Here's what happened…

In college, I knew computer science wasn't for me, but external pressures convinced me to finish the degree and get a job. 

So that was the plan. 

I grinded through my degree, graduated in under 4 years, and landed a six-figure Fortune 500 gig by the start of 2023\.

On paper, I had made it. In reality, I was miserable.

Spring 2023: My college roommate Abhi Das called. 

He'd been building an agency at Conduit, and wanted to take it to the next level. 

I'd watched him take big swings and risks for years. 

After 4 years of friendship, I trusted his judgment and we were off to the races.  
   
July 2023: I realized that the fulfillment I felt from entrepreneurship & sales traced back to my childhood. I’ve always been naturally drawn to people that needed support, leadership, and someone dependable in their journey.

August 2023: We ran a campaign targeted at the construction industry for our client at the time. 

Closed a $93K deal day one. We figured out exactly how to market & close deals in the construction industry. And that’s when we started to realize…

These builders were incredible at what they did. They knew how to run crews, manage multi-million dollar projects, and deliver quality work. But they rarely had a repeatable or long-term strategy to actually win the projects and build the firm they envisioned.

Summer 2024: We took everything we learned at Conduit and founded Sunrise Systems. 

The model was simple: they focus on building, we focus on bringing the dream clients & projects.

And we're just getting started.
