I’ve worked with 12+ commercial construction companies, from drywall to design-build contractors—some doing upwards of $20MM in annual revenue. I’ve never seen a company struggle to land high-paying projects when they applied these principles: 

Brand clarity: If clients can’t instantly see why you’re different, you’ll always compete on price.

Pipeline systems: A CRM, personal follow-ups, and consistent client check-ins run in the background while your crews are booked.

Pipeline discipline: Treat your bids like projects. If you track $10M onsite but nothing is queued up next, you’re already headed for a dry spell. 

Consistency over chaos: Don’t wait until you’re desperate to do sales or marketing. Keep the engine running while you’re busy.

90% of commercial companies I speak with don't do this and are stuck in a never-ending cycle of feast and famine. 

When you’re feasting, you’re too busy to think about sales and marketing.

When you’re famished, you scramble to find work, but by the time those efforts pay off, you’re buried in jobs again.

And the cycle repeats.

The only way out is building systems that keep opportunities flowing in the background, even when your hands are full.
