## DELIVERY NOTES FOR SPEAKER

**General Tone:**
- Conversational, like you're walking a colleague through your business
- Not rehearsed or overly polished - natural pauses are good
- Smile in your voice, be warm and approachable
- No hard sells, no urgency tactics
- Sound genuinely interested in helping, not closing a deal

**Pacing:**
- Speak at natural conversational pace (not rushed)
- Pause for 1-2 seconds when transitioning between major points
- Let the numbers on Delta and America 9 breathe - don't rush past them
- Total runtime should be 2:00-2:20 (gives you some flexibility)

**Emphasis Points:**
- Emphasize the RESULTS numbers (make them pop slightly)
- When saying "Growth Architecture," say it like you're introducing a concept they'll want to remember
- When delivering the CTA, sound inviting but casual - "we'd love to meet you" should feel genuine

**What NOT to Do:**
- Don't sound like you're reading a script
- Don't rush through the numbers
- Don't oversell or hype things up
- Don't use sales voice or urgency language
- Don't make it sound like they NEED to book - keep it optional and low-pressure

**Visual Cues:**
- Reference the slides as you go: "Here's what that looks like..." (when showing Delta)
- Use natural transitions: "Now...", "Here's how...", "For example..."
- Look at the camera like you're talking to one person, not a crowd

## FAQ SECTION (Optional - For Post-Video Page or Follow-Up)

**Q: What types of firms do you work with?**
A: We work exclusively with commercial contractors, design-build firms, and specialty trades doing $20M-$100M in annual revenue.

**Q: How much does it cost to work with you?**
A: Every Growth Architecture strategy is custom, so investment varies. The examples you saw (Delta at ~$1,300/month, America 9 at ~$3,400/month) give you a sense of the range. We'll show you exact pricing in your strategy session.

**Q: How long does it take to see results?**
A: Most clients start seeing qualified opportunities within the first 30-60 days. America 9 built a $6.5M pipeline in 90 days. Delta closed their first deal within 8 months and built $15M in pipeline.

**Q: Do you work with firms outside our region?**
A: Yes, we work with contractors across the US. Our sales team targets opportunities in your specific market, wherever that is.

**Q: What if we already have marketing or sales in-house?**
A: Great! We typically complement existing efforts rather than replace them. Our Growth Architecture approach fills pipeline gaps and creates predictability alongside what you're already doing.

**Q: Is this just lead generation?**
A: No. Lead gen is part of what our sales team does, but Growth Architecture includes marketing positioning and custom software too. It's an integrated growth strategy, not just leads.

## NOTES

- Script is written to feel natural when spoken, not when read
- Some sentences are deliberately fragmented or conversational
- Contractions are used throughout to maintain casual tone
- Numbers are repeated in different ways to ensure retention
- No jargon or industry buzzwords beyond what the audience already knows
- The CTA is deliberately soft and consultative
- The entire script avoids creating resistance or triggering "sales radar"
