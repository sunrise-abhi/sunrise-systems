**WHO WE ARE**

Delta Family Companies is an industry leader in innovative, enduring, high-quality, end-to-end commercial, residential, and industrial building in the architecture, engineering, and construction space.

Our dedication to craftsmanship and personalized service is built on more than 50 years of experience and knowledge passed down through generations of family ownership and operation.

**OUR TAGLINE**

**Built different.**

We are a family business descended from a line of black sheep. Figuring out different, better ways of doing things is in our DNA.

**OUR CORE BELIEF**

We believe that every structure and building should be a reflection of the society we want to live in.

That means obsessive attention to detail to ensure projects get done right, with structures that are built to last. It means smart decision making that benefits both our clients and the community. And it means fairness in every aspect of our business, from our relationship with clients to our relationship with employees. 

**OUR VISION**

Functional, efficient, well-designed buildings as the new standard.

The places where we work, eat, sleep, and spend time together are foundational to the individual, the community, and the greater collective. Dignity in every design and quality in every corner means a higher standard of living for everyone.

**OUR MISSION**

Our mission is to create spaces that enhance and inspire our communities through thoughtful design, obsessive craftsmanship, and rigorous trust.

We seek out partnerships with clients, subcontractors, and employees who share our sense of commitment and dedication. 

**OUR POSITIONING**

Delta Family Companies helps build stronger, more resilient communities by guiding business owners and local developers through the process of building functional, durable, well-designed structures and spaces, from concept to completion.

Our work isn’t simply contracting and construction. We pride ourselves on a hands-on, extremely thorough approach to every aspect of a project. It’s what we’ve built our reputation on.

**VALUE PROPOSITION**

We are experts, full stop; from the first conversation to flipping the open sign, we oversee every detail of every solution that gets the job done.

We pride ourselves on our relationships—not just with clients and team members, but with the city officials that issue approvals and permits. Strong working relationships mean faster, better, and more efficient projects.
