In a competitive market, opening six months ahead of your competition can be worth millions in revenue.

We learned that lesson in the best way during this Lancaster dispensary project. The client called us in November with what seemed like an impossible ask: **he wanted to be the first dispensary to open in the area, and asked if we could get him operational in six months.**

My gut reaction was that he was crazy. Six months is normally just the approval timeline, not the entire project.

But we made it happen anyway. Here's how:

* **Concurrent permitting strategy**: We submitted demo plans separately while the main TI plans were in review. This let us start demolition in December while still working through plan check.  
* **At-risk construction approach**: The client agreed to a cost-plus model, meaning if plans changed during approval (which they did), he'd cover the changes. This kept us moving instead of waiting.  
* **In-house MEP engineering**: We didn't have to wait on third-party consultants. When plan check comments came back, we addressed everything in one week instead of the usual back-and-forth delays.  
* **Aggressive follow-up**: We pushed the plan check reviewers every single week. Most contractors submit and wait; we stayed on top of every step.

The building was an old DMV that became a manufacturing facility, and we were converting it to retail. Change of use projects are always more complex because they trigger parking compliance, full ADA upgrades, and energy code requirements.

But our strategy worked. We got full approval in six weeks instead of the typical six months. The construction timeline was under two months once we had permits, but we'd been working during plan check.

**Total timeline from engagement to opening was just over five months.**

The result? This client opened months before any competitor and immediately started pulling in high six-figure monthly revenue. By capturing first-mover advantage, they're probably doing seven figures a month now.

The competitors didn't open for another six months after us.

That speed advantage was worth millions in market share and revenue that the client will never give up.
