When you hire separate architects, engineers, and contractors, you’re hiring three different teams with three different goals. 

These teams work under different contracts, with different priorities, and when things go wrong, they tend to point fingers instead of solving problems.

Design-build contractors (like Delta) reduce some of that chaos by handling everything under one roof – architecture, engineering, and construction – with one goal: finishing your project successfully and efficiently.

You work with **one team from start to finish**. 

* They design with construction in mind from day one.  
* They engineer solutions that fit your budget.  
* They build exactly what was planned, without surprises.

This means fewer delays, fewer change orders, and a smoother experience for you.

And it gives you a single point of accountability, so you always know who’s responsible.

The Design Build Institute of America claims that by 2028, design-build will account for almost half of U.S. construction spending – about $2.6 trillion – because these projects are delivered 102% faster on average and with 3.8% less cost growth than traditional methods.

So for a streamlined, less stressful construction process, **design-build is the way to go**.
