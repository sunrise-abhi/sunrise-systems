**Subject:** The governor cut red tape. Will it help you build faster?

In response to the LA wildfires, the governor announced efforts to **streamline the permitting process**. Which is great. But is it actually going to help things go faster?

The short answer is yes… but there are a lot of other factors that work into the permitting process.

Governor Newsom’s [recent executive order](https://www.nytimes.com/2025/01/12/us/newsom-ceqa-california-fires.html) waives two permits: the California Environmental Quality Act (CEQA) and the California Coastal Act, which *will* speed things up.

But the thing is, those permits are a fraction of the clearances required to get shovels in the ground. Here are some other **updates that will help you build faster:**

* The mayor also issued an [executive order](https://mayor.lacity.gov/news/mayor-bass-issues-sweeping-executive-order-clear-way-angelenos-rebuild-their-homes-fast) that expedites “like for like” rebuilding, meaning if you stay within your original footprint (or up to 10% larger), you can **skip planning reviews**. That’ll save you months—possibly *years*.   
* The Army Corps of Engineers is [**handling site cleanups for free**](https://recovery.lacounty.gov/debris-removal/phase-2/), so you don’t need to hire a private remediation crew. The deadline to apply is March 31; [fill out this form](https://recovery.lacounty.gov/debris-removal/roe/) to get started.
