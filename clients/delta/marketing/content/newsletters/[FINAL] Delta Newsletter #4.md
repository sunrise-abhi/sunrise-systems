**Subject: This tech helps you build faster & cheaper**

Before we started using BIM (short for **Building Information Modeling**), my assumption was that it was A.) expensive, and B.) overly complicated. There was a lot of buzz around it, but I was skeptical it could be more effective and efficient than old school building methods.

Turns out I was wrong.

BIM is mostly what it sounds like: a process that creates a 3D model of a building used to coordinate all disciplines. But it’s the *information* part that’s the big deal: Within the 3D model, there’s comprehensive data about **materials, dimensions, cost estimates, and performance characteristics** of each element.

Working with BIM (as opposed to traditional 2D plans, each broken out into their separate disciplines) makes it significantly easier for a team—architects, engineers, contractors, and owners—to collaborate and identify issues *months* before shovels hit the ground.

Why BIM is a real-deal game changer:

* **Improved collaboration**: All project participants work with the same model that updates in real time. This is a huge, huge help in minimizing miscommunications (hell yeah).   
* **More efficient use of time and $$**: Early detection of issues reduces costly rework, and accurate scheduling and cost estimation improve project timelines. It’s helped us put together accurate numbers over the course of a week, rather than months of detailed scoping and revisions.  
* **Smart sustainability modeling:** BIM allows for simulations that optimize building energy use, lighting design, material selection, and environmental impact.  
* **Data management:** The building model is a living document that evolves throughout the building's lifecycle. This is priceless info for future management, maintenance, and renovations.

Right now, only about 20% of architects and even fewer engineers consistently use this tech, but that's starting to change. And while it might cost a little more up front, it is—and I can’t emphasize this enough—**well worth it in the long run.** We’ve used it to save clients weeks (sometimes months) and tens of thousands (sometimes hundreds of thousands) of dollars.
